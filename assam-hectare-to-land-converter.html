Comprehensive Guide to Regional Land Area Measurements in India

Land measurement across India is structurally fragmented, relying deeply on age-old local units that change coordinates from state to state. While northern states operate on Bigha, Biswa, and Killa, eastern regions evaluate calculations in Kattha, Dhur, and Lessa. Down south, systems transition into Cent, Ankanam, and Ground. Shayan Smart Tools solves this complexity by integrating multi-state computational land mapping modules directly inside a seamless algorithmic platform.

State-Specific Ground Truth Data

Our platform maps dynamic unit variations across states like Uttar Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, and Kerala, eliminating mathematical rounding variations during critical boundary assessments.

Algorithmic Precision Engineering

Every sub-tool processes raw dimensions against calibrated land revenue constants, delivering dependable estimates for legal documentation reference, agricultural plotting, and commercial real estate pricing.

Multi-Triangle Calculations for Irregular ("Tedi-Medi") Boundaries

Real-world fields and plots are rarely shaped in perfect rectangles or squares. To calculate irregular, non-symmetrical, or "Tedi-Medi" zameen boundaries accurately, Shayan Smart Tools features specialized Advanced Polygon and Irregular Land Calculators.
