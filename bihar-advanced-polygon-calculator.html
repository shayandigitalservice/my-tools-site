1. Introduction to Irregular Land Formations in Bihar (Jamin ni Mapni Background)

Bihar state ke andar agriculture plots, rural fields aur urban lands ka structural layout aksar asymmetrical hota hai. Ganga-Gandak plane terrain, purane zamindari layouts aur local village boundaries ke karan yahan ki lagbhag 85% se zyada jameen perfectly square ya rectangle aakar me nahi milti. Jab kisi zameen ya khet me teen ya char se zyada aade-tirche corners hote hain, toh use geometric language me Polygon (बहुकोण) kaha jata hai. Aisi tedi-medi jameen ka area jab normal length × width formula se nikala jata hai, toh calculations galat aati hain jo baad me registry, partition ya mutation ke waqt bada vivad ban jati hain.

Bihar Revenue Department aur Bhumi jankari portal (Bihar Land Records Portal) ke guidelines ke mutabik, zameen ki kharid-bikri ya Khatian-Khasra verification ke waqt physical dimensions ka sateek assessment mandatory hai. Is dikkat ko door karne ke liye professional ameen (surveyors) **Multi-Triangle Polygon Geometric Mapping** system ka istemal karte hain. Shayan Smart Tools ka ye advance **Bihar Advanced Polygon Land Calculator** isi mathematical logic par kaam karta hai taaki hamare farmers aur property consultants bina kisi structural error ke sateek result paa sakein.

4. Geometric Methodology: The Power of Multi-Triangle Method and Heron's Formula

Kisi bhi aade-tirche multidimensional khet ka sateek area nikalne ka sabse vishwasniya tarika ye hai ki us pure khet ko diagonals draw karke alag-alag individual triangles me baant diya jaye. Jab aapko har ek triangle ki teeno sides (Side A, Side B, Side C) feet me pata chal jati hain, toh pure mathematics ka sabse powerfull rules Heron's Formula apply kiya jata hai:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

Hamara system background processing loop chalakar har ek triangle ka individually square feet area nikalta hai aur unhe aapas me merge (accumulate) kar deta hai. Uske baad automatic script processing ke jariye Bihar revenue framework ke mutabik unhe **Bigha, Kattha, Dhur, Acre, aur Hectare** me badal diya jata hai. Bihar me Kattha aur Dhur ka maap custom **Laggi length (लग्गी की लंबाई)** ke dynamic variable par constant run karta hai jo har district me badal sakta hai. 5. Landmark Identification Features: Bhumi jankari Bihar, Khasra, and Khatauni

Bihar me digital portal par apni land tracking records inspect karte waqt niche diye gaye teen key structural variables ko samajhna behad jaroori hai:

A. Bhumi jankari Portal (बिहार भूमि डिजिटल रिकॉर्ड): Bihar Government ka official land records software portal jahan har ek khet ka map, Jamabandi, Khasra aur Khatian records secure online verified status me host rehta hai.

B. Khasra Number (खसरा नंबर): Har ek agriculture land block ya plot ko mila ek unique identification map index number jiske bina geographic boundary traceability sambhav nahi hai.

C. Jamabandi / Khatauni Record (जमाबंदी): Ek ledger account file jo ye batati hai ki us Khasra number block ka aadhikarit malik (owner) kaun hai aur uski kul jameen kitni hai.

6. Top 15 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Bihar Land Revenue 2026

Q1. Bihar mein 1 Bigha mein kitna Kattha hota hai?
Ans: Bihar state revenue system ke mutabik 1 Bigha pure 20 Kattha ke barabar mana jata hai, aur 1 Kattha me 20 Dhur hote hain.

Q2. Bhumi jankari Bihar portal par official land maps kis format me hote hain?
Ans: Bihar Bhumi portal par digital naksha download karne par land boundaries Hectare, Acre aur Square meter metric criteria me mapped rehti hain.

Q3. Bihar me 1 Kattha me kitne Square Feet (वर्ग फुट) hote hain?
Ans: Bihar me Kattha ka size local Laggi (लग्गी की लंबाई) par depend karta hai. Standard 5.5 haath ki laggi par 1 Kattha me exact 1361.25 Square Feet area hota hai.

Q4. Bihar mein 1 Hectare mein kitne Acre hote hain?
Ans: Revenue conversion calculation matrix ke anusar 1 Hectare mein pure 2.471 Acres jameen hoti hai.

Q5. Tedi-medi jameen ka map nikalne ke liye Multi-Triangle calculator kyu best hai?
Ans: Jab boundary skew ya asymmetrical lines me ho, toh unka right-angle formula kaam nahi karta. Multi-triangle method pure polygon ko triangles me break karke zero margin error area deta hai.

Q6. Bihar me Laggi (लग्गी) ka maap district wise kyu badalta hai?
Ans: Local land revenue traditions ke anusaar alag-alag aanchal me laggi 4 haath se 8 haath tak hoti hai, par Sarkari Mapi (Standard Revenue) me 5.5 haath manya hai.

Q7. 1 Dhur me kitne Square Feet hote hain (5.5 Haath Laggi)?
Ans: Standard 5.5 haath laggi (8.25 feet) ke mutabik 1 Dhur = (8.25)^2 = 68.0625 Sq Ft hota hai.

Q8. Bihar me Ameen jameen mapi ke liye Heron's Formula kyu use karte hain?
Ans: Trigonometry ka Heron's formula bina kisi 90-degree angle requirement ke kisi bhi aakar ke trikon ka exact area nikal leta hai.

Q9. Kya 1 Acre me Bihar ke standard 32 Kattha hote hain?
Ans: Haan, 5.5 haath laggi par 1 Acre (43,560 Sq Ft) lagbhag 32 Kattha (1 Bigha 12 Kattha) ke barabar banta hai.

Q10. Bihar Land Mutation (Dakhil Kharij) me polygon calculation kyu zaroori hai?
Ans: Mutation process ke waqt physical possession area aur khatian area me antar na aaye, iske liye polygon mapi behad sateek mani jati hai.

Q11. Multi-triangle method me diagonal measurements kaise lein?
Ans: Boundary ke ek corner se opposite corner tak chain ya tape se sidhi line maapein jo khet ko do aamne-saamne ke trikon me baante.

Q12. Square feet se Sq Meter me land convert karne ka formula kya hai?
Ans: Square Feet area ko 10.7639104 se bhag (divide) karne par sateek Square Meters mil jata hai.

Q13. Bihar me 4 Haath ki Laggi par 1 Kattha kitna hota hai?
Ans: 4 haath (6 feet) laggi me 1 Dhur = 36 Sq Ft aur 1 Kattha = 720 Sq Ft hota hai.

Q14. Gaya aur Southern districts me 6 Haath Laggi se Kattha kitna banta hai?
Ans: 6 haath (9 feet) laggi par 1 Dhur = 81 Sq Ft aur 1 Kattha = 1,620 Sq Ft hota hai.

Q15. Kya is Bihar Polygon Calculator Report ko PDF me print karke save kiya ja sakta hai?
Ans: Haan! 'Print Report' par click karke aap official diagnostic layout ko PDF me save kar sakte hain.
