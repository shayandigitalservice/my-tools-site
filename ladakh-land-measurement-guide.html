Ladakh Sa-Tse Tsang-ma Kaihhruaina 2026: Kanal, Marla, Sq Ft, Sq Mtr bad Acre Conversions

Sngon-jod bad Ladakh-gi Sa-tse Rig-gnas Background

Ladakh UT zhes-pa ri-bo mthon-po bad rong-khul skya-bo-i nang-na chags-ste yod-pa-i sa-cha khyad-par-can zhig yin. Htsang-ma-i dus-su Ladakh nang-na sa-i che-chung zhing-kha-i 'Zhing' bad 'Khal' (khals-kha zhing-kha) tar-la tsed-de yod. Yang-da lta UT Ladakh Revenue Department bad zhing-khul khag-la Kanal, Marla, Sarsai, Square Feet (Sq Ft) bad Square Meters (Sq Mtr) zhes-pa-i sa-tse thuran mdzad-de yod.

Kumin 2026 steng-la Leh, Kargil, Nubra, Zanskar bad Changthang-gi sa-i bdag-po-i deb (Revenue Records) bad sa tsong-mna' mdzad-pa-i dus-su, local unit Kanal bad Marla-po National standards (Square Feet / Meters) mnyam-po dik-po chhut-stangs hses-pa shin-tu gal-chen yin. Sa-i khram (Registry), Jamabandi, Revenue Survey (Bhu-Naksha Ladakh) bad Bank Loan-gi don-du sa-tse tshad-ma hses-pas rtsod-pa med-pa tsang-ma 'byung-ngo.

Sa-Tse Chhut-Stangs Mathematics Formula bad Solved Example

Ladakh-na sa-i khang-pa bad zhing-kha-i che-chung dik-po chhut-pa-la, a-ma-nas sa-i ring-thung (Length) bad zhen-thung (Width) Feet nang-na tsed-gos.

Mathematics Formula Sa-Tse Chhut-Stangs:
Sa-i Che-Chung (Square Feet) = Ring-Thung (Feet) × Zhen-Thung (Feet)

Marla Chhut-Stangs = Total Square Feet ÷ 272.25
Kanal Chhut-Stangs = Total Square Feet ÷ 5,445 (yang-na Total Marla ÷ 20)
Acre Chhut-Stangs = Total Square Feet ÷ 43,560

Chawhpawlh Dpe (Solved Example):

Dper-na, Leh-i Choglamsar yang-na Kargil-na sa-i plot ring-thung Feet 90 bad zhen-thung Feet 60 yod-pa zhig yin-na:

  • Step 1 (Square Feet Chhut): 90 Feet × 60 Feet = 5,400 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.)
  • Step 2 (Square Meters Chhut): 5,400 ÷ 10.764 = 501.67 Square Meters
  • Step 3 (Marla Chhut): 5,400 ÷ 272.25 = 19.83 Marla
  • Step 4 (Kanal Chhut): 5,400 ÷ 5,445 = 0.992 Kanal (Kanal 1-la chha-nyung chik med-pa)

Local Terminology bad UT Ladakh Revenue Rules 2026

Ladakh UT Revenue Department (Deputy Commissioner / Tehsildar Office) nang-na hman-pa-i gzhung-gi min-khag:

  • Jamabandi (Khasra Girdawari): Sa-i bdag-po-i ming, sa-tse Kanal-Marla-na bad khasra number 'bri-sa-i official ledger book.
  • Intiqal (Mutation): Sa tsong-mna' mdzad-pa-i rjes-la bdag-po ming hsar-pa revenue record-la 'goy-stangs.
  • Khasra Number: Land Revenue Survey-na sa-i plot re-re-la phul-ba-i official identification number.
  • Aks-Sajra (Bhu-Naksha): Revenue Department-gi sa-i map bad boundary outline.

Kumin 2026 UT Ladakh Revenue Policy: UT Ladakh administration-gis GIS bad DGPS-gi thog-nas sa khram-stangs tsang-ma digitalized mdzad-de yod. ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) phul-te sa-i boundary Square Meters bad Feet-na dik-por 'bri-gos.
