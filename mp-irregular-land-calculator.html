15 Short & Powerful FAQs - मध्य प्रदेश जमीन की पैमाइश (MP Revenue Rules 2026)

Q1. MP mein 1 traditional Bigha mein kitne Square Feet hote hain?

Ans: MP revenue standard scaling ke mutabik 1 Bigha mein exactly **12,000 Sq.Ft** hote hain.

Q2. 1 Kattha aur 1 Dhur ka map value kya hai?

Ans: MP mein 1 Kattha = **600 Sq.Ft** aur 1 Dhur = **30 Sq.Ft** hota hai.

Q3. 1 Bigha mein kitne Kattha aur Dhur hote hain?

Ans: 1 Bigha mein **20 Kattha** aur kul **400 Dhur** hote hain.

Ans: Area = $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-e)}$, jahan $s = (a+b+e)/2$ (Semi-perimeter) hota hai.
