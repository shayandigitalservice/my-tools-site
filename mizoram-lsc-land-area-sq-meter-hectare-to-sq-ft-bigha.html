Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you convert LSC pass Square Meters into Square Feet and Bighas in Mizoram?
Multiply LSC pass Square Meters by 10.7639 to get Square Feet (1 Sq. Meter = 10.7639 Sq. Ft.), and divide Square Feet by 14,400 (or divide Square Meters by 1,337.81) to get Mizoram Bighas (1 Mizoram Bigha = 14,400 Sq. Ft. = 1,337.81 Sq. Meters).
Q: How do you convert Hectares into Square Feet and Bighas for Mizoram LSC records?
1 Hectare = 10,000 Sq. Meters = 1,07,639 Sq. Ft. = 7.4748 Bighas (2.471 Acres).
Q: How many Square Feet and Bighas are in 1,337.81 Square Meters from an LSC pass?
1,337.81 Square Meters × 10.7639 ≈ 14,400 Sq. Ft., which equals 1.00 Mizoram Bigha (10 Traditional Tin).
