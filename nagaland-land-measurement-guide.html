Nagaland Mati Mapikilibole Formula 2026: Sq Ft, Sq Mtr, Bigha, Acre aru Hectare Conversion

Nagaland Laga Mati Mapi Kilibo Niyam aru Background

Nagaland state te mati ownership aru mapikilibo laga system toh bura India laga bura jagah nisina nahoy. Amakhan laga state te **Article 371A** under te customary law aru Village Council khan laga hat te bura power thake. Dimapur, Chumoukedima aru plain area te Bigha, Katha, Less, Square Feet aru Acre chali thake. Pahad jaga khan (Kohima, Mokokchung, Tuensang, Wokha, Mon) te mati toh Village Council, Clan nohole Individual ownership under te thake.

2026 laga modern time te, mati kinibole, bechibole, Patta/Land Settlement Certificate (LSC) bonabole nohole Bank loan te apply kuribole tailor kuria hisab lagonye **Square Feet, Square Meters, Acre aru Hectare** te sahi conversion janitu bhabana bisat dorkar ase. Nagaland te mati mapikilibo time te mistake nahobole sob manu khan eitu conversion formula thik kene bujibole lage.

Step-by-Step Mati Hisab Kuribo Formula (Calculation)

Nagaland te konba plot nohole khiti mati mapi kilibole prothom te length (dighal) aru width (chiya/chotai) Feet te mapi kilibole lage.

Pura Mathematics Formula:
Total Area (Square Feet) = Length (Feet) × Width (Feet)

Acre Hisab = Total Square Feet ÷ 43,560
Bigha Hisab (Plain Area) = Total Square Feet ÷ 14,400
Square Meters Hisab = Total Square Feet ÷ 10.764

Example Solved (Kene hisab kuribo example):

Bhabana kuribi Chumoukedima nohole Dimapur te ekta plot length 120 Feet aru width 60 Feet ase:

  • Step 1 (Square Feet nikalibi): 120 Feet × 60 Feet = 7,200 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.).
  • Step 2 (Bigha te convert kuribi): 7,200 ÷ 14,400 = 0.5 Bigha (Aadha Bigha / 2.5 Katha).
  • Step 3 (Acre te convert kuribi): 7,200 ÷ 43,560 = 0.165 Acres.
  • Step 4 (Square Meters te convert kuribi): 7,200 ÷ 10.764 = 668.9 Square Meters.

Nagaland Local Words aru Government Rules 2026

Nagaland Land Revenue Department aru District Administration te chali thaka main words:

  • Land Settlement Certificate (LSC) / Patta: Government le issue kuria official document ji te mati owner laga naam aru plot boundary thake.
  • Village Council Clearance: Nagaland te konba mati bechibole/kinibole prothom te Village Council laga No Objection Certificate (NOC) dorkar hoye.
  • Article 371A Rights: Indian Constitution laga special power, ji te Nagaland tribal mati non-tribal manu khan te bechibole allowed nahoy.
  • Dag / Plot Number: Land Revenue Survey record te plot identify kuribole number.

2026 Digital Niyam: Nagaland Revenue Department abhi DGPS survey aru GIS Satellite Mapping system use kurine Land Records LSC computerize kuria chali ase. Registration time te Sq Ft aru Sq Mtr hisab thik kene dorkar hoye.

Sodai Hodha Hudhi Khan (FAQ)

Hudhi: Nagaland te 1 Acre te kintu Square Feet thake?
Jawab: Nagaland te 1 Acre mati mane 43,560 Square Feet (4,046.86 Sq. Mtrs.) hoye.
Hudhi: Dimapur plain area te 1 Bigha te kintu Square Feet thake?
Jawab: Dimapur aru plain area te 1 Bigha mati mane 14,400 Square Feet hoye.
Hudhi: Non-tribal manu Nagaland te mati kinibole pare na?
Jawab: Article 371A aru Nagaland Land Protection Rules under te, non-indigenous/non-tribal manu khan tribal mati kinibole allowed nai.
Hudhi: 1 Hectare te kintu Acre thake?
Jawab: 1 Hectare te 2.47 Acre (10,000 Square Meters) thake.
