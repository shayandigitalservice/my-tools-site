Mizoram Ram Teh Dan Kaihhruaina 2026: Sq Ft, Sq Mtr, Tin, Acre leh Hectare Conversions

Thuhmahruai leh Mizoram Hnam Chin Ram Teh Dan History

Mizoram hi tlangram leh zoram duhawm tak a ni a, hmasang Mizo pi leh pute atangin huan leh lo ram teh dan bawk hrang hrang kan lo hmang thin a ni. Hmasang ah chuan 'Tin' (buhfai tin khat tuhna) leh 'Lo' tihte hmangin lo zau zawng chhut a ni thin. Mahse, sawrkar leh sumdawnna ah chuan **Square Feet (Sq Ft), Square Meters (Sq Mtr), Acre, Hectare leh Bigha** tihte a lo tul tak zawk avangin tualchhung hmang leh sawrkar teh dan a lo inthlau ta a ni.

Kumin 2026 thleng hian Aizawl, Lunglei, Champhai leh Mizoram khaw hrang hrangah ram leilawn, in hmun ram thut leh in inhren transaction hrang hrangah Revenue & Land Settlement Department hnuaia Land Settlement Certificate (LSC) leh Periodic Pass te chu dik taka **Square Feet leh Acre** a chhut luh a ngai thin. Ram inrochun leh inchuh lak a kan hmin a, Revenue Record (Bhu-Naksha Mizoram) a felfai taka awm theih nan ram chhut dan dik tak hriat hi Mizo mi tinte mawhphurhna a ni.

Karena Chhutna Mathematics Formula leh Example

Mizoramah in hmun neih emaw, huan ram zau zawng dik tak chhut chhuah nan a dung (Length) leh a vang (Width) te Feet in teh hmasak ber tur a ni.

Mathematics Formula Zau Zawng Chhut Dan:
Ram Zau Zawng (Square Feet) = Dung (Feet) × Vang (Feet)

Acre a chhutna = Total Square Feet ÷ 43,560
Square Meters a chhutna = Total Square Feet ÷ 10.764
Tin (Bigha equivalents) = Total Square Feet ÷ 14,400

Entirna Chawhpawlh (Solved Example):

Pindana in hmun emaw Aizawl chhehvela ram a dung 120 Feet leh a vang 90 Feet a zau a awm a nih chuan:

  • Step 1 (Square Feet chhut): 120 Feet × 90 Feet = 10,800 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.).
  • Step 2 (Square Meters a lehlawn): 10,800 ÷ 10.764 = 1,003.3 Square Meters.
  • Step 3 (Acre a lehlawn): 10,800 ÷ 43,560 = 0.247 Acres.
  • Step 4 (Tin / Bigha a lehlawn): 10,800 ÷ 14,400 = 0.75 Tin (Bigha thum ah hmun li).

Local Terminology leh Mizoram Revenue Rules 2026

Mizoram Land Revenue & Settlement Department hnuaia tawngkam pawimawhte:

  • LSC (Land Settlement Certificate): Ram neitu nihna nghet tura Sawrkar Land Revenue Department-in Certificate a pek a ni.
  • Periodic Pass: Huan emaw In hmun tura hun tiang bika sawrkarin phalna lekha a pek, kum tin renewal ngai a ni.
  • Dung leh Vang Boundary (Pillar/Rik): Ram ri bipekna lung phun emaw border tehna hmun marker.
  • MLR Act (Mizoram Land Revenue Act): Mizoram ram inenkawlna leh ram chhiahtirna hrang hrang enkawltu dan bu.

Kumin 2026 Digital Revenue Policy: Mizoram Sawrkar chuan DGPS (Differential Global Positioning System) leh GIS mapping hmangin LSC zawng zawngte Digital Boundary Coordinates thothang in a thlunpui mek a, ram inleina ah Sq Ft leh Sq Mtr a tarlan ngei a tul a ni.

Zawt Zin Bik Sual Inzawmte (FAQ)

Zawhna: Mizoramah Ram 1 Acre-ah Square Feet engzat nge don?
Tawngtu: Mizoram ram teh dan dik takah 1 Acre hi 43,560 Square Feet (4,046.86 Sq. Mtrs) a ni.
Zawhna: 1 Square Meter-ah Square Feet engzat nge awm?
Tawngtu: 1 Square Meter ah hian 10.764 Square Feet a awm a ni.
Zawhna: LSC leh Periodic Pass danglamna eng nge ni?
Tawngtu: LSC hi ram neitu nihna tak tak (Permanent Ownership) a ni a, Periodic Pass erawh hun tiang bika phalna lekha a ni.
Zawhna: 1 Hectare-ah Acre engzat nge awm?
Tawngtu: 1 Hectare ah hian 2.47 Acre vel (10,000 Square Meters) a awm a ni.
