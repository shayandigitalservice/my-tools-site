Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do land measurement and rules differ between Imphal Valley and Hill Districts in Manipur?
Imphal Valley uses surveyed patta records, Sangam (27,225 Sq. Ft. / 0.625 Acre), Pari (4 Sangam = 2.5 Acres), and metric/Sq. Ft. plot measurements governed under the MLR & LR Act, whereas Hill Districts have distinct topography, hilly/irregular plot shapes, and local/customary land arrangements.
Q: What are the basic rules under the Manipur Land Revenue and Land Reforms Act (MLR & LR Act)?
The MLR & LR Act provides the statutory framework for land revenue administration, survey/settlement, patta/land records, land use/diversion rules, and transfer/occupancy regulations in covered areas of Manipur.
Q: How many Square Feet are in 100, 200, and 500 Sq. Meter urban plots in Imphal?
Using 1 Sq. Meter = 10.764 Sq. Ft.: 100 Sq. Meter = 1,076.40 Sq. Ft., 200 Sq. Meter = 2,152.80 Sq. Ft., and 500 Sq. Meter = 5,382.00 Sq. Ft.
