Chibai! Mizoram Land Partition & LSC Transfer Rules 2026: How to Calculate Land Share (Ram Insem)

Chibai! Mizoram-a ram intawm then dan (Ram Insem) leh LSC transfer hunah ram zau zawng pum (total area) atanga chanvo hrang hrang chhut chhuah dan hi a pawimawh hle. Ram zau zawng pum hi unit khatah — entirnan Tin (1 Mizoram Bigha = 10 Tin; 1 Traditional Tin = 1,440 Sq. Ft.) emaw Sq. Ft. (1 Mizoram Bigha = 14,400 Sq. Ft.) — dah hmasa la, chumi hnuah inzat-a intawm zat (number of equal sharers) emaw chanvo zat (share ratio/percentage)-in sem/pun rawh.

💡 Chibai! Example: Splitting 2 Bigha 5 Tin (25 Tin) Among 4 Equal Sharers (Ram Insem)

  • Step 1 — Total Tin chhut rawh (× 10): (2 × 10) + 5 = 25 Tin (36,000 Sq. Ft.)
  • Step 2 — Chanvo pakhat (4 inzat): 25 ÷ 4 = 6.25 Tin chanvo pakhat zelah
  • Step 3 — Sq. Ft. & Bigha-ah: 6.25 × 1,440 = 9,000 Sq. Ft. = 0.625 Bigha
  • 50% (1/2) chanvo entirna: 25 Tin × 0.5 = 12.5 Tin (1 Bigha 2.5 Tin) = 18,000 Sq. Ft.

Ram Insem (Land Share) Calculation Formulas

Ram zau zawng pum atanga chanvo chhut chhuahna formula pawimawhte:

Core Formulas:
Total Tin = (Total Bigha × 10) + Total Tin; Total Sq. Ft. = Total Tin × 1,440
Share Tin = Total Tin ÷ Number of Sharers (or Total Tin × Share %)
Share Sq. Ft. = Share Tin × 1,440; Share Bigha = Share Sq. Ft. ÷ 14,400

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you calculate each sharer's portion (Ram Insem) from total land area in Mizoram?
Convert total area into a common unit such as Tin (1 Mizoram Bigha = 10 Tin; 1 Traditional Tin = 1,440 Sq. Ft.) or Sq. Ft. (1 Mizoram Bigha = 14,400 Sq. Ft.). Divide by the number of equal sharers or multiply by each sharer's share ratio/percentage, then convert back into Bigha, Tin, or Sq. Ft.
Q: In a worked example, what is each of 4 equal sharers' portion from 2 Bigha 5 Tin (25 Tin)?
2 Bigha 5 Tin = (2 × 10) + 5 = 25 Tin = 36,000 Sq. Ft. Divided among 4 equal sharers, each gets 25 ÷ 4 = 6.25 Tin = 9,000 Sq. Ft. (0.625 Bigha).
Q: What key checks apply to Mizoram land partition and LSC transfer?
Verify the LSC/survey record, total area, co-sharers' consent/partition agreement, sketch/boundary alignment, and applicable transfer/revenue rules with the concerned authority.
