1. Introduction to Irregular Land Formations in Chandigarh (Jamin ni Mapni Background)

Union Territory of Chandigarh state ke andar sector plots, commercial layout plots, aur suburban village areas (jaise Mani Majra, Dhanas, Hallomajra, Behlana) ka structural layout aksar asymmetrical hota hai. Modern town planning ke sath-sath purane village abadi deh boundaries, corner plots aur peripheral agricultural lands ke karan yahan ki zameen perfectly rectangular ya square aakar me nahi milti. Jab kisi zameen ya plot me teen ya char se zyada aade-tirche corners hote hain, toh use geometric language me Polygon (बहुकोण) kaha jata hai. 2. Geometric Methodology: The Power of Multi-Triangle Method and Heron's Formula

Kisi bhi aade-tirche multidimensional plot ka sateek area nikalne ka sabse vishwasniya tarika ye hai ki us pure plot ko diagonals draw karke alag-alag individual triangles me baant diya jaye. Jab aapko har ek triangle ki teeno sides (Side A, Side B, Side C) feet me pata chal jati hain, toh pure mathematics ka sabse powerfull rules Heron's Formula apply kiya jata hai:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

Hamara system background processing loop chalakar har ek triangle ka individually square feet area nikalta hai aur unhe aapas me merge (accumulate) kar deta hai. Uske baad automatic script processing ke jariye Chandigarh UT revenue framework ke mutabik unhe **Kanal, Marla, Sarsai, Acre, aur Hectare** me badal diya jata hai. 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Chandigarh Land Records

Q1. Chandigarh mein 1 Kanal mein kitne Marle hote hain?
Ans: Chandigarh land revenue rules aur standard northern metric criteria ke mutabik 1 Kanal mein pure 20 Marle hote hain.

Q2. Chandigarh Estate Office aur revenue records par official land measurement units kaun si hain?
Ans: Chandigarh UT mein official revenue, property registration, aur sector plots mapping ke liye Kanal-Marla, Square Feet, Square Yards (Gaj), aur Hectare Metric system ka upayog kiya jata hai.

Q3. Chandigarh mein 1 Marla mein kitne Square Feet (वर्ग फुट) hote hain?
Ans: Chandigarh state revenue framework ke mutabik 1 standard Marla exact 272.25 Square Feet ke barabar hota hai, jabki 1 Kanal 5,445 Square Feet ke barabar hota hai.

Q4. Chandigarh mein 1 Acre mein kitne Kanal aur Marle hote hain?
Ans: Standard Chandigarh revenue matrix ke mutabik 1 Acre mein exact 8 Kanal (yani 160 Marle) zameen hoti hai jo ki 43,560 Square Feet ke barabar hai.

Q5. Asymmetrical ya irregular sector plots ke liye Multi-Triangle calculator kyu best hai?
Ans: Corner plots ya irregular polygon shapes par basic length × width formulas fail ho jate hain. Multi-Triangle method overall field ko triangles mein split karke Heron's formula dwara exact zero margin error result deta hai.
