Boundary & Diagonal Area Formula (Heron's Formula)

Ki formula ba kongsan ban khein ia ka jingiar ka plot lum bad ba ym long kylleng:

Core Formulas:
Semi-perimeter (s) = (side1 + side2 + diagonal) ÷ 2
Triangle Area (Sq. Ft.) = √[s × (s - side1) × (s - side2) × (s - diagonal)]
Total Sq. Ft. = Area(Triangle 1) + Area(Triangle 2); Acre = Total Sq. Ft. ÷ 43,560

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you calculate the area of a hilly or irregular plot in Meghalaya from four sides and a diagonal?
Measure all four boundary sides and one diagonal in feet to split the plot into two triangles. Calculate each triangle's area using Heron's formula (s = (a+b+c)/2, Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]) and add them to get total Square Feet.
Q: Why is measuring the diagonal important for hilly boundary sketches?
Four boundary lengths alone do not fix the interior angles of an irregular plot. Measuring the diagonal fixes the exact geometry of both triangles so the area is accurate.
Q: How do you convert total Square Feet into Acres and Square Meters?
Divide total Square Feet by 43,560 to get Acres (1 Acre = 43,560 Sq. Ft.) and divide by 10.764 to get Square Meters (1 Sq. Meter = 10.764 Sq. Ft.).
