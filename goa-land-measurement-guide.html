Introduction and Land Measurement Tradition in Goa

In Goa, 1 square meter is precisely equal to 10.764 square feet (and 1 Are is equal to 100 square meters). Goa has a unique land revenue and survey system heavily influenced by its Portuguese historical legacy (such as the Cadastral survey and Matriz records). Unlike many other Indian states that rely primarily on traditional units like Bigha or Kathas, Goa's official land records, sale deeds, and real estate transactions uniformly use the metric system—specifically Square Meters (Sq. Mtrs.), Ares, Hectares, and Square Feet.

💡 Quick Fact Box (Goa Land Conversions):

  • 1 Square Meter = 10.764 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.)
  • 1 Are = 100 Square Meters = 1,076.39 Square Feet
  • 1 Acre = 40.47 Ares = 4,046.86 Square Meters = 43,560 Square Feet

Step-by-Step Calculation Formula & Solved Example

To accurately compute the total area of any rectangular plot or land parcel in Goa, measure both the length and width in meters or feet.

Mathematical Formula:
Total Area (Square Meters) = Length (Meters) × Width (Meters)

Square Feet Conversion = Total Square Meters × 10.764
Ares Conversion = Total Square Meters ÷ 100
Acres Conversion = Total Square Meters ÷ 4,046.86

Solved Practical Example:

Suppose you own a plot in Goa with a length of 30 meters and a width of 20 meters.

  • Step 1 (Find Square Meters): 30 m × 20 m = 600 Square Meters (Sq. Mtrs.)
  • Step 2 (Convert to Square Feet): 600 × 10.764 = 6,458.4 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.)
  • Step 3 (Convert to Ares): 600 ÷ 100 = 6 Ares
  • Step 4 (Convert to Acres): 600 ÷ 4,046.86 = 0.148 Acres

Local Terminology & Goa Revenue Rules 2026

Key terms used by the Directorate of Settlement and Land Records (DSL) and the Goa Revenue Department:

  • Form I-XIV: The primary land record document in Goa detailing survey numbers, sub-divisions, legal ownership, and exact areas in square meters/ares.
  • Index II: A registrar document that provides details of past property transactions, sale deeds, and market values.
  • Matriz (Land Registration): Portuguese-era land register records maintained alongside modern survey numbers.
  • Mutation (Nomantorn): The legal procedure of updating property ownership records in Form I-XIV following a sale or inheritance.

2026 Digital Revenue Policy: The Government of Goa has integrated DGPS and GIS mapping across talukas, making digital survey plans and Form I-XIV online verification transparent and mandatory for property registrations.
