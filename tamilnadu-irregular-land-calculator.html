    2. Heron's Formula Triangulation Mechanism Explained

    Jab zameen ki chaaron bhujaayein (Side A, B, C, D) alag-alag lambai ki hoti hain, toh use quadrilateral (chaturbhuj) kehte hain. Iska area nikalne ke liye ham geometric triangulation method ka upyog karte hain. Diagonal (E) ke zariye yeh plot do alag triangles ($T_1$ aur $T_2$) mein bat jata hai. Har triangle ka ardh-parimap (Semi-perimeter $s$) nikal kar Heron's formula lagaya jata hai:

    Semi-perimeter $s = (a + b + e) / 2$
    Triangle Area = $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-e)}$
    Total Plot Area = Area($T_1$) + Area($T_2$)

    Is advanced mathematical calculation se zameen ka ek-ek square feet bilkul sateek taur par nikal kar samne aata hai, jisse Patta Chitta portal ya survey records ke sath mismatch hone ki koi gunjaish nahi rehti.
