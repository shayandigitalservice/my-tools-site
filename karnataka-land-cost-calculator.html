1. How to Use the Karnataka Land Registry Calculator (Step-by-Step Guide)

Managing property acquisitions in Karnataka requires strict numerical clarity to ensure compliance with the Department of Stamps and Registration. Follow this straightforward procedure to determine your total budget instantly:

  1. Input Parcel Area: Enter the size of your land parcel into the Land Area Value field (e.g., 5 Guntas or 0.25 Acres).
  2. Select the Unit: Choose your local measurement unit from the dropdown menu: Guntas (ಗುಂಟಾ), Acre (ಎಕರೆ), or Square Feet (ಚದರ ಅಡಿ).
  3. Specify Unit Price (₹): Enter the agreed market rate or official Sub-Registrar guideline rate per unit (e.g., ₹5,00,000 per Gunta).
  4. Select Jurisdiction Region: Choose between BBMP / BDA Zone (includes 10% Surcharge + 2% Infrastructure Cess), Other Urban / Town (10% Surcharge), or Rural / Gram Panchayat (3% Surcharge).
  5. Choose Property Slab Bracket: Select the correct property value range (Above ₹45 Lakhs = 5% Stamp Duty; ₹20L - ₹45L = 3%; Below ₹20L = 2%).
  6. Enter Survey / RTC Number (Optional): Provide your official Bhoomi Pahani or Survey number for audit logging.
  7. Click Calculate Budget: Instantly inspect your complete itemized breakdown, interactive plot shape preview, and export options.

Mandatory Documents Required for Land Registration in Karnataka

  • Bhoomi RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops) / Pahani
  • Property Card & Survey Sketch (Form 11E / Tatkal Podi)

  • Government Approved ID Proofs (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card)

Example A: BBMP Area Corporation Grid Allocation

  • Input Dimensions: 2 Guntas Area value evaluated at ₹25,00,000 per Gunta guideline base rate.
  • Pure Land Market Cost: 2 * ₹25,00,000 = ₹50,00,000.
  • Stamp Duty Tier Assessment (>45L): 5% of ₹50,00,000 = ₹2,50,000.
  • BBMP Surcharge & Cess Structure: (10% Surcharge + 2% Cess = 12% total over Stamp Duty) = 12% of ₹2,50,000 = ₹30,000.
  • Registration Charges: 1% of Land Cost = ₹50,00,000 * 1% = ₹50,000.
  • Total Estimated Net Budget: ₹50,00,000 + ₹2,50,000 + ₹30,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹53,30,000.

4. Top 15 Short Q&A FAQs - Karnataka Land Records & Kaveri 2.0

Q1: What is the land registration fee in Karnataka?
Ans: Standard registration fee pure land transaction cost ka flat 1% hota hai.

Q2: How much is stamp duty in Bangalore/BBMP area?
Ans: ₹45 Lakhs se upar ki property par 5% stamp duty + 10% surcharge + 2% cess lagta hai.

Q3: What is Kaveri 2.0 portal?
Ans: Yeh Karnataka government ka official online portal hai jahan property registration aur valuation hoti hai.

Q4: 1 Gunta is equal to how many square feet in Karnataka?
Ans: Revenue records ke anusar Karnataka me 1 Gunta exact 1,089 Sq.Ft ke barabar hota hai.

Q5: How can I check land valuation online in Karnataka?
Ans: Official Kaveri Online Services web portal par jakar property details enter karke check kar sakte hain.

Q6: What is the stamp duty for rural land in Karnataka?
Ans: Rural areas me 5% stamp duty ke sath keval 3% panchayat surcharge lagta hai (cess zero hota hai).

Q7: Is PAN card compulsory for property registration in Karnataka?
Ans: Haan, ₹5 Lakhs se adhik transaction value par PAN card ya Form 60 mandatory hai.

Q8: How many Guntas make one Acre in Karnataka?
Ans: Karnataka system standard ke mutabik full 40 Guntas se ek standard Acre banta hai.

Q9: What is the stamp duty slab for below ₹20 Lakhs property?
Ans: Entry-level relief scheme ke under ₹20 Lakhs se kam ki properties par base stamp duty rate sirf 2% hai.

Q10: Can I calculate stamp duty on market value?
Ans: Stamp duty hamesha Government Guideline value ya actual Sale value, dono me se jo bhi zyada ho, uspar calculate hoti hai.

Q11: What is the role of Bhoomi portal?
Ans: Bhoomi framework digital RTC (Pahani) certificate download karne aur mutation validation tracking ke kaam aata hai.

Q12: Is there any concession for women buyers in Karnataka?
Ans: Filhal urban corporate properties par uniform rates hain, concessional slabs budget brackets par fix hain.

Q13: What is 11E sketch map?
Ans: Kisi land parcel ke subdivision validation ke liye cross verify hone wala revenue survey department ka map hota hai.

Q14: How to get Encumbrance Certificate (EC) online?
Ans: Aap Kaveri 2.0 web login ke madhyam se search parameters fill karke certified digitally signed EC download kar sakte hain.

Q15: What is surcharge on stamp duty?
Ans: Surcharge ek structural revenue percentage assessment hai jo local urban town development/infrastructure funds ko process karta hai.
