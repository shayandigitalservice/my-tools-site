Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you convert sketch map Square Meters into Square Feet and Acres for KHADC & GHADC records?
Multiply sketch map Square Meters by 10.764 to get Square Feet (1 Sq. Meter = 10.764 Sq. Ft.), and divide Square Meters by 4,046.85 (or divide Square Feet by 43,560) to get Acres (1 Acre = 43,560 Sq. Ft. = 4,046.85 Sq. Meters).
Q: What is the role of the GHADC Patta Management System and KHADC Land Holding Certificate?
Under Sixth Schedule provisions in Meghalaya, KHADC deals with Land Holding Certificates and related records in Khasi Hills jurisdiction, while GHADC oversees patta records, survey/sketch details, and patta administration through the GHADC Patta Management System in Garo Hills.
Q: How many Square Feet and Acres are in 4,046.85 Square Meters from a sketch map?
4,046.85 Square Meters × 10.764 ≈ 43,560 Sq. Ft., which equals 1.00 Acre.
Q: Which calculators can be used to convert Meghalaya sketch map and patta areas?
You can use the Meghalaya Land Area Calculator, Meghalaya Acre to Square Feet, Meghalaya Hectare to Acre, Meghalaya Katha to Square Feet, and Meghalaya Bigha to Square Feet.
