Required Documents for Property Registry in Chandigarh UT

Chandigarh Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) aur Estate Office par property registration deed execution ke time in documents ki requirement hoti hai:

  • Original Sale Deed Draft (Vikray Patra / Transfer Deed).
  • Identity Proof documents (Jaise Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, ya Passport).
  • Address Proof Documents Buyer aur Seller dono parties ke liye.
  • Estate Office Allotment Letter / No Dues Certificate (NDC).
  • e-Stamp Duty Receipt printout aur Online Registration Fees Payment Receipt.
  • Two passport size photographs of all parties (Buyer, Seller, and Witnesses).
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) and Building Plan Sanction Copy if applicable.
