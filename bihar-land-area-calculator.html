Introduction to Bihar Land Mapping System (2026 Standards)

Bihar me jab bhi aap kisi plot, krishi zameen (agricultural farmlands), commercial boundaries, ya housing property ka khareed-bikray (buying and selling) karte hain, toh sabse pehle us zameen ka sateek Land Area Calculator run karna anivarya hota hai. Bihar State Revenue Department (Bhumi Jankari & Bihar Bhumi Portals) ke local land mapping rules pure Bharat me sabse alag aur traditional maane jaate hain. Yahan ka land measurement system mukhya roop se Bigha, Kattha, Dhur, Dhurki, Decimal (Dismil), Acre aur Hectare units par completely base hota hai.

Aapko pata hona chahiye ki Bihar Sarkar ke official digital registry registers me area hamesha Acre, Decimal aur Hectare me record kiya jata hai, par local zameeni khareed-farokht me aaj bhi Bigha, Kattha, aur Dhur hi sarvopari hain. Bihar land measurement me sabse critical variable Laggi (लग्गी) hoti hai. Laggi ek traditional baans (bamboo stick) matrix scale hai jiski lambai alag-alag jilon (districts) aur parganas me badalti rehti hai. Laggi ki lambai badalne se 1 Dhur aur 1 Kattha ka Square Feet area automatically badal jata hai. Is specialized multi-unit laggi integrated algorithm core module ko software developer Muksidul Islam dwara build kiya gaya hai taaki Bihari nagrik bina kisi ganit bhool ke sateek registry calculation kar sakein.

Understanding Local Bihar Revenue Unit Metrics & Laggi System

Zameen ki lambai (Length) aur chaudai (Width) feet ya meter me mapne ke baad niche diye gaye units ko samajhna zaroori hai:

1. Laggi (Haath ki Naap) System Connection: Bihar revenue tracking manuals ke mutabik standard laggi 5.5 Haath (hands) ki hoti hai jo Patna, Danapur, Muzaffarpur, Gaya aur Central Bihar me baseline hoti hai. Kuch North Bihar jilon (Darbhanga, Madhubani) me 6, 6.5 ya 7 haath ki laggi use hoti hai. 1 Haath me exact 1.5 Feet hote hain. Is prakar 5.5 haath ki laggi = 8.25 Feet hoti hai. 1 Dhur ka formula (Laggi in Feet)² hota hai. Standard 5.5 haath ki laggi par 1 Dhur holds exactly 68.0625 Square Feet.

2. Kattha & Bigha Mathematical Rules: Bihar conversion rules me fixed hierarchy hoti hai: 20 Dhur = 1 Kattha. Standard 5.5 haath laggi par 1 Kattha me flat 1,361.25 Square Feet hote hain. Theek isi tarah 20 Kattha = 1 Bigha, jisme total 27,225 Square Feet ka area banta hai.

3. Decimal & Acre Matrix Bounds: Government Registry Portal (MVR) ke anusar 1 Decimal / Dismil = 435.6 Square Feet hota hai. Absolute legal standard compliance ke tehat 1 Acre = 100 Decimal (43,560 Sq Ft) hota hai, jo 5.5 haath laggi par exact 1.60 Bihar Bigha ke barabar hota hai.

Step-by-Step Practical Calculation Example

Maan lijiye aapne Bihar me ek plot dekha jiski lambai aur chaudai niche di gayi hai:

  • Measured Length (Lambai): 200 Feet
  • Measured Width (Chaudai): 136.125 Feet
  • Laggi Selection: 5.5 Haath (Standard Scale)

Is numeric entry par hamara converter pipeline calculation step-by-step kaise evaluate karta hai:

Step 1: Gross Surface Area = Length × Width
Total Area = 200 ft × 136.125 ft = 27,225 Square Feet (Sq.Ft)

Step 2: Calculate 1 Dhur Sq Ft = (5.5 × 1.5)² = (8.25)² = 68.0625 Sq Ft
Total Dhur Count = 27,225 / 68.0625 = 400 Dhur

Step 3: Convert to Kattha = 400 / 20 = 20 Kattha

Step 4: Convert to Bigha = 20 / 20 = 1.00 Bigha

FINAL OUTPUT REPORT: 1 Bigha, 0 Kattha, 0 Dhur Total Plot Area.
