1. Land Measurement Metrics in UT Chandigarh

Union Territory Chandigarh mein land administration aur estate records mein properties alag-alag units mein darshayi jati hain. Urban residential aur commercial plots ko Square Yards (Gaj) ya Square Feet mein calculate kiya jata hai, jabki rural ya outer sectors mein Kanal aur Marla system apply hota hai.

2. Standard Revenue Metric Formulae (UT Chandigarh)

  • 1 Marla: Flat 272.25 Square Feet (30.25 Gaj / 25.29 Sq. Meters).
  • 1 Kanal: Exact 20 Marla = 5,445 Square Feet (605 Gaj).
  • 1 Acre: Exact 8 Kanal = 160 Marla = 43,560 Square Feet.

💡 Conversion Example

Agar aapke paas 2 Kanal, 5 Marla, aur 100 Gaj zameen hai:

  • 2 Kanal = 2 × 5,445 = 10,890 Sq. Ft.
  • 5 Marla = 5 × 272.25 = 1,361.25 Sq. Ft.
  • 100 Gaj = 100 × 9 = 900 Sq. Ft.
  • Total Area = 13,151.25 Sq. Ft. (2 Kanal & 8.24 Marla)
