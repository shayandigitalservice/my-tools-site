1. Introduction to Irregular Land Formations in Rajasthan (Zameen ki Napai Background)

Rajasthan state ke andar agriculture plots, rural fields aur urban lands ka structural layout aksar asymmetrical hota hai. Thar desert terrain, purane jagirdari layouts aur local village boundaries ke karan yahan ki lagbhag 85% se zyada jameen perfectly square ya rectangle aakar me nahi milti. Jab kisi zameen ya khet me teen ya char se zyada aade-tirche corners hote hain, toh use geometric language me Polygon (बहुकोण) kaha jata hai. Aisi tedi-medi jameen ka area jab normal length × width formula se nikala jata hai, toh calculations galat aati hain jo baad me registry, partition ya mutation ke waqt bada vivad ban jati hain.

Rajasthan Revenue Department aur Apna Khata E-Dharti portal ke guidelines ke mutabik, zameen ki kharid-bikri ya Jamabandi verification ke waqt physical dimensions ka sateek assessment mandatory hai. 4. Geometric Methodology: The Power of Multi-Triangle Method and Heron's Formula

Kisi bhi aade-tirche multidimensional khet ka sateek area nikalne ka sabse vishwasniya tarika ye hai ki us pure khet ko diagonals draw karke alag-alag individual triangles me baant diya jaye. Jab aapko har ek triangle ki teeno sides (Side A, Side B, Side C) feet me pata chal jati hain, toh pure mathematics ka sabse powerfull rules Heron's Formula apply kiya jata hai:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

Hamara system background processing loop chalakar har ek triangle ka individually square feet area nikalta hai aur unhe aapas me merge (accumulate) kar deta hai.
