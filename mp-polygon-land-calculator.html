Madhya Pradesh Land Revenue Code (MPLRC 1959 / 2026 Revision) aur Revenue Department (MP Bhu-Abhilekh) ke guidelines ke mutabik, zameen ki kharid-bikri ya Jamabandi / Khasra-Khatoni verification ke waqt physical dimensions ka sateek assessment mandatory hai. 2. Geometric Methodology: Multi-Triangle Method & Heron's Pure Formula

Kisi bhi aade-tirche multidimensional plot ya khet ka sateek area nikalne ka sabse vishwasniya tarika ye hai ki us pure plot ko diagonals draw karke alag-alag individual triangles me baant diya jaye. Jab aapko har ek triangle ki teeno sides (Side A, Side B, Side C) feet me pata chal jati hain, toh pure mathematics ka sabse powerful rule Heron's Formula apply kiya jata hai:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

3. MP Land Revenue Code (MPLRC 2026 Guidelines) & Cadastral Mapping Rules

Madhya Pradesh me krishi aur gair-krishi (commercial/residential) zameen ke simankan aur batware ke liye Tehsil level par Revenue Officers (Tahsildar & Naib Tahsildar) aur Halka Patwari dwara Cadastral Survey maps ka sahara liya jata hai. MP Land Revenue Code (MPLRC) Section 165 aur Section 170 ke tahat zameen ki naksha-tarmim aur mutation tabhi swikar hoti hai jab bhu-naksha aur mauke ki maap (physical ground survey) me zero margin error ho.

Purane samay me Patwari Gunter's Chain (जरीब) dwara maap karte the, jisme 1 Chain = 66 Feet hoti thi. Jab kisi irregular polygon khet me tirchhi diwarein (diagonal boundaries) hoti hain, toh traditional 'औसत लंबाई × औसत चौड़ाई' (Average Length × Average Width) ka niyam fail ho jata hai aur isse area me 5% se 15% tak ki badi truti (error margin) paida ho jati hai.

Is truti se bachne ke liye modern Revenue Software Multi-Triangulation Algorithm par run karte hain. Jab aap khet ko alag-alag trikono me baantte hain, toh diagonal lines A-B, B-C aur C-A geometric stability pradan karti hain. Shayan Smart Tools ka MP Calculator visual drawing canvas aur exact Heron's mathematical equation se har ek trikon ka alag-alag Kshetrafal nikal kar zero-error Bigha-Bissa result deta hai. Isse registry, bank loan valuation, parivariv batwara, aur court case vivado me ek solid diagnostic proof milta hai.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Top 15 MP Land Records & Measurement Standards

Q1. Madhya Pradesh mein 1 Pakka Bigha mein kitne Square Feet hote hain?
Ans: MP State Land Revenue rules 2026 ke mutabik 1 Pakka Bigha me exact 27,225 Sq.Ft. होते हैं।

Q2. Madhya Pradesh mein 1 Bigha mein kitne Bissa (Biswa) hote hain?
Ans: MP Revenue framework me 1 Pakka ya Kachha Bigha me exact 20 Bissa (Biswa) hote hain.

Q3. MP me 1 Bissa mein kitne Dhur (Biswansi) hote hain?
Ans: MP me 1 Bissa me exact 20 Dhur (jise local bhasha me Biswansi bhi kaha jata hai) hote hain.

Q4. Madhya Pradesh mein 1 Hectare mein kitne Pakka Bigha hote hain?
Ans: Standard MP Revenue matrix ke anusaar 1 Hectare (1,07,639 Sq.Ft) me lagbhag 3.95 Pakka Bigha hote hain.

Q5. MP mein 1 Acre mein kitne Pakka Bigha hote hain?
Ans: 1 Acre (43,560 Sq.Ft) me Madhya Pradesh standard par exact 1.6 Pakka Bigha zameen hoti hai.

Q6. Madhya Pradesh me Kachha Bigha ki kitni value hoti hai?
Ans: Regional Kachha Bigha belt me 1 Kachha Bigha = 13,612.5 Sq.Ft (Pakka Bigha ka thik aadha) hota hai.

Q7. Malwa aur Nimar Kshetra me Bigha ka kya maap chalta hai?

Q8. Asymmetrical ya irregular aade-tirche plot ki napni kaise karein?

Q10. 1 Square Meter mein kitne Square Feet hote hain?
Ans: 1 Square Meter me exact 10.7639 Square Feet hote hain.

Q11. Khasra Mutation aur Registry me Bigha ki ganna kaise hoti hai?

Q13. 1 Dhur (Biswansi) mein kitne Square Feet hote hain MP me?
Ans: MP Pakka Bigha standard me 1 Dhur (Biswansi) exact 68.06 Sq.Ft ka hota hai.
