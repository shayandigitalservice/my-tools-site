Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you convert DLRS RoR Square Meters into Square Feet and Nagaland Bighas?
Multiply Square Meters by 10.7639 to get Square Feet (1 Sq. Meter = 10.7639 Sq. Ft.), and divide Square Feet by 14,400 (or divide Square Meters by 1,337.8) to get Nagaland Bighas (1 Bigha = 14,400 Sq. Ft. = 1,337.8 Sq. Meters).
Q: How do you convert DLRS RoR Hectares into Bighas, Acres, and Square Feet in Nagaland?
1 Hectare = 2.471 Acres (7.475 Bigha) = 1,07,639 Sq. Ft. Multiply Hectares by 7.475 for Bighas or by 1,07,639 for Square Feet.
Q: How many Square Feet and Bighas are in 1,337.8 Square Meters?
1,337.8 Square Meters × 10.7639 ≈ 14,400 Sq. Ft., which equals 1.00 Nagaland Bigha (5 Katha or 100 Lessa).
