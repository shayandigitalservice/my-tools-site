Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the main difference between Ri-Kynti and Ri-Raid land in Meghalaya?
Ri-Kynti land is privately owned/inherited or family/clan land with transferable/heritable rights subject to customary and legal rules, whereas Ri-Raid land is community/raid land meant for use/occupation by members of the raid/community subject to customary authority rules and restrictions on permanent sale/transfer.
Q: How is property valuation calculated for stamp duty and registration in Meghalaya?
Property valuation is based on the total measured area (in Sq. Ft., Sq. Meter, Acre, Hectare, Bigha, or Katha) and the applicable rate/consideration, and stamp duty and registration charges are verified against prevailing rules and the registering authority.
Q: What should be checked before land transfer or registration in Meghalaya?
Verify whether the land is Ri-Kynti or Ri-Raid, check holding/patta/sketch records and traditional/council clearances where applicable, and confirm stamp duty and registration charges.
