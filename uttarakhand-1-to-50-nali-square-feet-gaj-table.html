मुख्य सूत्र (Core Formulas):
Square Feet (Sq. Ft.) = Nali × 2,160
Gaj (Sq. Yards) = Nali × 240 (1 Gaj = 9 Sq. Ft.)
Mutthi = Nali × 16 (1 Mutthi = 135 Sq. Ft.)
