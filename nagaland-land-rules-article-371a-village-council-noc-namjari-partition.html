Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does Article 371A affect land ownership and transfer in Nagaland?
Article 371A of the Constitution of India protects Naga customary law and procedure, including matters relating to ownership and transfer of land and its resources, unless the Nagaland Legislative Assembly decides otherwise by resolution.
Q: How do land rules in the Dimapur cadastral area compare with Hill Districts in Nagaland?
Dimapur cadastral area has formal survey/patta/cadastral records and revenue/mutation procedures, whereas Hill Districts rely heavily on customary/clan/community tenure, Village Council NOCs, and traditional/local practices.
Q: What are the basic steps in the Namjari (mutation) and partition process in Nagaland?
First calculate and verify each sharer's area/hissa, obtain required Village Council NOC or supporting documents where applicable, submit the Namjari application with title/transfer/partition documents and sketch/area details, and complete verification and record updation with the concerned authority.
