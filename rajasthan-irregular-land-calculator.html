Rajasthan mein zameen ki napai aur bhumi prabandhan ka tarika kafi dilchasp aur geocentric hai. Yahan Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Bikaner, Sikar, Kota aur Alwar jaise alag-alag mandlo mein rajashwa vibhag (Apna Khata Rajasthan portal) par official dastawez Hectare mein darj hote hain, lekin jab baat gramanchal mein parivarik khetton ke batware ya tehsil sub-registrar ke yahan manual registries ki aati hai, toh log hamesha paramparik standard units jaise Bigha, Biswa, aur Biswansi mein hi calculations karte hain. Ek hi khate ke kai alag-alag khasra plots (scattered land patches) ka total jod nikalte samay haath se ya aam calculator se fraction units (biswa aur biswansi) ko calculate karne par galti hone ka darr bohot zyada rehta hai, jiski wajah se kisanon ko banking loan ya kanooni mutation ke samay kafi pareshani jhelni padti hai. एवरेज निकालने का पुराना फार्मूला गणितीय रूप से पूरी तरह गलत होता है और सीमाओं में विवाद पैदा करता है।

Is processing calculation load aur layout measurement error ko door karne ke liye Shayan Smart Tools ne is advanced Rajasthan Irregular Land Calculator ko completely mathematical triangulation engine standard par build kiya hai. Yeh tool Heron’s Formula ($Area = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$) ka upyog karta hai. Dono triangles ka area alag-alag nikal kar james jab unhe add kiya jata hai, toh pure plot ka area 100% sateek nikalta hai.
