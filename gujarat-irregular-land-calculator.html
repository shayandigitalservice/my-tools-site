    Gujarat mein agriculture fields holdings aur shehri residential plots ka survey records kafi unique aur non-symmetrical borders ke sath maps me darj hota hai across major districts such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Gandhinagar, Bhavnagar, Jamnagar, Junagadh, Anand, Mehsana, Navsari, Valsad, and Kutch.     2. Technical Core: The Mathematics of Diagonal Division and Heron's Formula

    Tedi-medi 4-konne wali zameen ka sateek blueprint area map calculate karne ka sabse ancient aur 100% scientifically certified path banta hai pure asset space ko ek corner to corner line use karke do clear triangles me divide kar dena. Is intersection center line ko legal metric terms me hum Diagonal / Tirchi Line (કર્ણ) bolte hain. Jaise hi aap nakshe ya field maps coordinates par diagonal track complete kar lete hain, complex four-sided entity layout do individual operational triangles sections me divide ho jati hai. Geometry laws ke criteria me jab dono individual triangles ki complete dimensions (Side A, Side B, aur common diagonal Side E) sateek mil jayein, toh double processing loops me Heron’s Formula apply kiya jata hai:

    s = (a + b + c) / 2

    Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

    Hamara system code processing environment back-end calculation matrix execution par dono split elements data structures area evaluate karke unhe dynamically secure accumulate (sum up) karta hai. Final absolute area value derive hone ke baad use standard metrics parameters conversion constants ratios ke anusar automatic standard units (Vigha, Guntha) me flat execute kar deta hai.
