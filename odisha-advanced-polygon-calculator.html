2. Geometric Methodology: Triangulation Engine and Heron's Formula

Complex geometric plots ka map accurate calculate karne ka absolute globally approved method hai complex asset block ko discrete right or scalable triangles me turn kar dena. Jab aap plot map boundaries par diagonals lines design karte hain, toh single complex plot data multiple mathematical sub-units me break ho jata hai. Teeno side parameters data variables (Side A, Side B, Side C) feed hote hi backend framework Heron’s Formula apply kar deta hai:

$$s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}$$

$$\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)}$$

Humara algorithmic processor backend loop runtime par single triangle metrics calculate karke unhe aggregate karke final output balance derive karta hai. 7. Practical Land Measurement Calculation Examples

Example 1 (Single Triangle Field): Maan lijiye aapke paas ek triangular plot hai jiski Side A = 100 Ft, Side B = 120 Ft, aur Side C = 150 Ft hai.

  • Semi-perimeter ($s$) = $(100 + 120 + 150) / 2 = 185\text{ Ft}$
  • $\text{Area} = \sqrt{185 \times (185-100) \times (185-120) \times (185-150)} = \sqrt{185 \times 85 \times 65 \times 35} \approx 5,979.8\text{ Sq. Ft.}$
  • In Gunta = $5,979.8 / 1,742.4 = \mathbf{3.43\text{ Gunta}}$

Example 2 (Complex 4-Corner Polygon with Diagonal): Ek 4-corner plot ko ek diagonal khinch kar 2 Triangles me toda gaya:

  • Triangle 1: 60 Ft, 80 Ft, 100 Ft $\rightarrow \text{Area} = 2,400\text{ Sq Ft}$
  • Triangle 2: 100 Ft, 120 Ft, 140 Ft $\rightarrow \text{Area} \approx 5,878.7\text{ Sq Ft}$
  • Total Plot Area: $2,400 + 5,878.7 = 8,278.7\text{ Sq Ft}$ ($\approx \mathbf{4.75\text{ Gunta}}$ ya $\mathbf{0.19\text{ Mana}}$)
