Understanding Local Rajasthan Units

1. Pacca Bigha vs Kaccha Bigha: Eastern aur Central Rajasthan (Jaipur, Alwar, Dausa) me **Pacca Bigha** (27,225 Sq. Ft.) chalta hai. Western Rajasthan (Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Barmer) me **Kaccha Bigha** (9,075 Sq. Ft.) chalta hai. 1 Pacca Bigha me exact **3 Kaccha Bigha** hote hain.

2. Biswa & Biswansi: 1 Pacca Bigha me **20 Biswa** hote hain. 1 Biswa ka size **1,361.25 Sq. Ft.** hota hai. 1 Biswa ke andar 20 Biswansi hoti hain.

3. Hectare & Acre Base: Official Jamabandi me 1 Hectare ko **2.471 Acres** ya **3.954 Pacca Bigha** ke barabar maana jata hai.
