Introduction to Rajasthan Land Measurement Rules 2026

Rajasthan me kisi bhi zameen, kheti (agricultural plot), ya property ki khareed-beech ke waqt sateek land area calculation sabse zaroori pehlu hai. State Revenue Department (Apna Khata E-Dharti Portal) ke anusar, Rajasthan me official Registry aur Jamabandi me area **Hectare** me likha hota hai, jabki local level par **Pacca Bigha, Kaccha Bigha, Biswa, aur Gaj (Sq. Yard)** ka istemal hota hai.

Isse registry, intakal (mutation), ya e-Panjiyan stamp valuation ke waqt hone wali galtiyan bilkul khatam ho jaati hain.

Practical Calculation Example (सटीक उदाहरण)

Maan lijiye aapke paas Rajasthan me ek kheti ka plot hai jiski naap niche di gayi hai:

Calculated Area Steps:
• Total Sq. Ft = 165 ft × 165 ft = 27,225 Sq. Ft.
• Pacca Bigha = 27,225 ÷ 27,225 = 1.00 Pacca Bigha
• Biswa = 1 Pacca Bigha = 20 Biswa
• Square Yards (Gaj) = 27,225 ÷ 9 = 3,025 Gaj
• Hectare = 27,225 ÷ 1,07,639 = 0.2529 Hectare

New Digital Revenue & Land Mutation Rules Rajasthan 2026

Rajasthan Government Revenue Rules 2026 ke tehat **e-Dharti digital portal** aur **GIS Land Mapping Framework** ko complete update kiya gaya hai. Naye niyam ke mutabik sabhi Khasra numbers aur Jamabandi Mutation records ko exact Hectare-to-Bigha geo-tagging matrix se joda gaya hai. Iska matlab hai ki ab kisi bhi plot ya kheti ki naap me 0.01 Sq. Feet ki bhi galti hone par registry hold ho sakti hai.

Shehri kshetro (Jaipur Development Authority - JDA, Jodhpur Development Authority, Kota Urban) me registry hamesha **Square Yards (Gaj)** aur **Square Feet** me hoti hai, jabki gramin kshetro me **Pacca Bigha** aur **Hectare** ko hi legal validation milta hai. Hamara ye tool Rajasthan 2026 ke naye GIS mapping rules ke anusar hi sateek calculation return karta hai.
