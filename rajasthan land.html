Introduction to Rajasthan Land Mapping Rules 2026

Rajasthan me kisi bhi agricultural land, plot, ya residential farmland ki khareed-bech aur registry se pehle **Land Area Calculator** chalana sabse zaroori step hota hai. Rajasthan Revenue Department ke updated 2026 digital land records guidelines (Apna Khata E-Dharti) ke mutabik state ka measurement framework baaki neighboring states se bilkul alag hai. Rajasthan me DGPS (Differential Global Positioning System) survey rules 2026 ke tehat land area calculation me thodi si bhi galti registry rejection ka kaaran ban sakti hai. Understanding Local Rajasthan Revenue Unit Metrics (2026 Standard)

Land Length (Lambai) aur Width (Chaudai) ko Feet, Meter, ya Gaj me maapne ke baad units ka exact relationship samajhna zaroori hai:

1. Hectare & Acre Standard Base: Rajasthan revenue records (Jamabandi E-Dharti) me 1 Hectare me exact **2.471 Acres** ya **1,07,639 Square Feet** (11,960 Sq. Yards / Gaj) hote hain.

2. Pacca Bigha vs Kaccha Bigha: Eastern Rajasthan (Jaipur, Alwar, Bharatpur) me **Pacca Bigha** (27,225 Sq. Ft.) chalta hai. Jabki Western & Southern Rajasthan (Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Udaipur) me **Kaccha Bigha** (9,075 Sq. Ft.) use hota hai. Exact rule ke mutabik **1 Pacca Bigha = 3 Kaccha Bigha** hota hai.

3. Biswa & Biswansi Metrics: 1 Pacca Bigha me exact **20 Biswa** hote hain. Phir 1 Biswa me **1,361.25 Square Feet** aate hain, aur 1 Biswa aage **20 Biswansi** units me divide hota hai.
