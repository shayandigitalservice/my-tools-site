India's Complete Land Area & Regional Unit Conversion Guide

Measuring land in India can be complex due to the diversity of regional land units used across different states. While the metric system (Square Meters, Hectares) and standard Imperial units (Square Feet, Acres) are recognized nationally, local real estate transactions, legal property documents (Khatian, Jamabandi, 7/12 extractor), and agricultural surveys heavily rely on traditional units such as Bigha, Kattha, Biswa, Gunta, Ground, and Cent.

At Shayan Smart Tools, we provide high-precision, state-specific land calculators designed according to exact government standards across all 28 states and Union Territories of India.

North Indian Land Measurement Units

In states like Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh, land is primarily measured in Bigha, Biswa, Biswansi, Killa, and Kanal. Because the exact size of a Bigha varies from state to state (and even district to district), our calculators adjust dynamically to regional land survey standards.

South Indian Land Measurement Units

Southern states including Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Kerala commonly use units like Cent, Ground, Ankanam, Gunta, and Cents. Precise conversion to Square Feet is crucial here for urban plot buying and construction estimations.

Eastern & North-Eastern Regional Units

States such as West Bengal, Assam, Odisha, Jharkhand, and the North-Eastern hilly terrains utilize units like Lessa, Satak, Chatak, and Dhur. Our algorithms accommodate regional irregular land (Tedi-Medi Zameen) shapes using advanced polygon and multi-triangle formulas.
