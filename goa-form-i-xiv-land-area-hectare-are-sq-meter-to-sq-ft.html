Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: In Goa Form I & XIV, how is an area entry like 0 - 05 - 50 calculated into Square Meters and Square Feet?
0 - 05 - 50 represents 0 Hectares, 5 Are, and 50 Sq. Meters. Since 1 Are = 100 Sq. Meters, 5 Are = 500 Sq. Meters, plus 50 Sq. Meters = 550 Sq. Meters. Multiplying by 10.7639 gives 550 × 10.7639 = 5,920.15 Sq. Ft. (5,920.145 Sq. Ft.).
Q: What do Survey Number and Sub-division represent in Goa Form I & XIV?
The Survey Number identifies the main surveyed land parcel/plot in the village record, while the Sub-division number identifies a specific divided portion or plot within that survey number.
Q: What is the difference between Form I and Form XIV in Goa land records?
Form I and Form XIV are key Goa land revenue record forms containing survey/sub-division numbers, occupant/owner and tenant/cultivation details, land classification/nature, and area entries in Hectare-Are-Sq. Meter.
