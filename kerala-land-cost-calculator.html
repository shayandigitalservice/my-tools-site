2. Practical Mathematical Computation Examples

Real estate financial frameworks ko acche se samajhne ke liye chaliye do practical examples ko theoretical layout matrices ke adhar par samajhte hain:

Example A: Cent Sizing Region Acquisition (Ernakulam Urban District)

  • Input Dimensions: 5 Cent Area mapped at ₹5,00,000 per Cent baseline rate.
  • Pure Land Market Cost: 5 * ₹5,00,000 = ₹25,00,000.
  • Stamp Duty Assessment (8%): 8% of ₹25,00,000 = ₹2,00,000.
  • Local Body Cess (1%): 1% of ₹25,00,000 = ₹25,000.
  • Registration Charges (2%): 2% of ₹25,00,000 = ₹50,000.
  • Total Estimated Net Budget: ₹25,00,000 + ₹2,00,000 + ₹25,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹27,75,000.
