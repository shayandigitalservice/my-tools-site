Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do you calculate each co-owner's share (hissedari) from Form I & XIV area in Goa?
Convert the total Form I & XIV Hectare - Are - Sq. Meter area into total Sq. Meters or Sq. Ft., then divide by the number of equal co-owners or multiply by each co-owner's share ratio/percentage, and verify that all shares add up to the total area.
Q: What should be checked regarding stamp duty and registration charges in Goa?
Stamp duty and registration charges depend on property valuation, instrument/deed type, location/classification, and prevailing Goa stamp duty rules, so confirm applicable rates and slabs from the sub-registrar/revenue authority before executing documents.
Q: What is distinctive about Communidade land in Goa?
Communidade land is traditional community-managed land governed by specific Communidade regulations, zonn/concession/lease rules, and administrative approvals, which differ from ordinary private patta/Form I & XIV holdings.
