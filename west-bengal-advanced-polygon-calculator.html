2. Geometric Methodology: The Power of Multi-Triangle Method and Heron's Formula

Irregular polygon plots ka sateek framework map evaluate karne ka sabse ancient aur globally certified system hai bada asset patch ko chhote independent right/irregular triangles me convert kar dena. Jab aap apni tedi zameen ke dastawez ya ground map coordinates par diagonals lines draw karte hain, toh complex data boundaries discrete triangles blocks me slice down ho jati hain. Geometric patterns me jab kisi triangle ki teeno sides data variables (Side A, Side B, Side C) completely secure mil jayein, toh geometry ka sabse powerfull calculation standard Heron’s Formula apply kiya jata hai:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

Area = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - West Bengal Land Records & Area Converter

Q1. West Bengal-e 1 Bigha koto Katha hoy? (পশ্চিমবঙ্গে ১ বিঘা = কত কাঠা?)
Ans: West Bengal revenue guidelines ebong standard bhumi map anusare 1 Bigha = Exactly 20 Katha (২০ কাঠা) hoy.

Q2. West Bengal-e 1 Katha koto Sq Ft? (১ কাঠায় কত বর্গফুট?)
Ans: West Bengal local land measurement law anusare 1 Katha = Exactly 720 Square Feet (৭২০ বর্গফুট).

Q3. 1 Chatak-e koto Sq Ft hoy West Bengal-e? (১ ছটাকে কত বর্গফুট?)
Ans: 1 Katha = 16 Chatak, tai 1 Chatak = Exactly 45 Square Feet (৪৫ বর্গফুট).

Q4. 1 Satak ba Decimal-e koto Sq Ft hoy? (১ শতক বা ডেসিমলে কত বর্গফুট?)
Ans: West Bengal Banglarbhumi standard conversion ratio anusare 1 Satak (Decimal) = Exactly 435.6 Square Feet (৪৩৫.৬ বর্গফুট).

Q5. 1 Acre-e koto Bigha hoy West Bengal-e? (১ একর = কত বিঘা?)
Ans: West Bengal unit-e 1 Acre = Exactly 3.025 Bigha (3 Bigha 1 Katha 4 Chatak) ebong 100 Satak/Decimal.

Q6. Tedi-medi ba irregular jami kivabe naapbo? (অসম বহুভুজ জমি মাপার নিয়ম?)
Ans: Jami-ke multiple Triangles-e bhag kore Heron's Formula (Side A, B, C) diye zero error sateek area ber kora jay.

Q7. Banglarbhumi West Bengal portale area kon unit-e thake?
Ans: Government land portal Banglarbhumi-te area hamesha Acre ebong Satak / Decimal (শতক) format-e save thake.

Q8. West Bengal land record-e Khatian number ki? (খতিয়ান নম্বর কি?)
Ans: Khatian Number holo zamidar ba bhumi maliker unique digital indexing registration code (LR/RS Khatian).

Q9. Daag Number (দাগ নম্বর) bolte ki bojhay?
Ans: Daag Number holo survey dwara nirdharito nirdisht jomir plot-er geo-referenced unique tracking number.

Q10. 1 Bigha-te koto Square Meter thake? (১ বিঘা = কত বর্গমিটার?)
Ans: Metric standard scale anusare 1 Bigha = Exactly 1,337.8 Square Meters (বর্গ মিটার).

Q11. Online ROR Khatian kivabe check korbo Banglarbhumi-te?
Ans: West Bengal Banglarbhumi portal-e giye District, Block, Mouza ebong Khatian Number select kore submit korun.

Q12. Shayan Smart Tools Polygon Calculator output kitna reliable hai?
Ans: Yeh mathematical Heron's formula based dynamic engine par customized hai jo 100% precise area calculate karta hai.

Q13. 1 Acre-te koto Square Feet thake? (১ একর = কত বর্গফুট?)
Ans: Standard land measurement rules anusare 1 Acre = Exactly 43,560 Square Feet (১০০ শতক).

Q14. West Bengal land unit hierarchy sequence ki?
Ans: Hierarchy sequence holo: Acre -> Satak/Decimal -> Bigha -> Katha -> Chatak -> Sq Ft.
