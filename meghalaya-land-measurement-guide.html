Meghalaya Adkhar Thei Khyndew Jingkyntiew 2026: Sq Ft, Sq Mtr, Acre bad Hectare Conversions

Lympung ha Meghalaya bad ki Rukom Thei Khyndew Tynrai

Ka Jylla Meghalaya ka don kynja spah ba kyrpang ha ka rukom synshar bad ka jinglong trai khyndew. Wadon ka aiñ ka ri India ba khein ha ki Hectare bad Square Meter, ha Meghalaya pat ka rukom thei khyndew ka shong ha ka aiñ Sixth Schedule katkum ki District Councils (KHADC ha Khasi Hills, JHADC ha Jaintia Hills bad GHADC ha Garo Hills). Ha ka rukom khein ka Khasi Hills, ia ki khyndew ki khein kham bun da ki **Square Feet (Sq Ft), Acre, Hectare bad Square Meters**.

Ka rukom trai khyndew Meghalaya ka kylla namar ka bhah ia ki khyndew ha ki **Ri-Kynti** (Khyndew ba don trai shimet) bad **Ri-Raid** (Khyndew Shnong ne Syiem). Ha u snem 2026, man ba don ka jingthied ne jingshend khyndew ha Shillong, Nongpoh, Jowai ne Tura, ka long ba kordor ia ki trai khyndew bad nongthied ban sngewthuh beit ia ki rukom khein Square Feet bad Acre khnant ban ym don ka jingbakla ha ka syrnot Land Holding Certificate (LHC) ne Bhu-Naksha Revenue Records.

Rukom Khein bad ki Sutro Mathematiks (Calculation Formula)

Ban khein ia ka Jingiar bad ka Jingjngai ba beit jong ka pynthor ne u plot khyndew ha Meghalaya, shim ia ka jingjrong (Length) bad ka jingiar (Width) da ki Feet.

Sutro Khein Kynja Mathematiks:
Jingiarlap (Square Feet) = Jingjrong (Feet) × Jingiar (Feet)

Khein ha Acre = Total Square Feet ÷ 43,560
Khein ha Square Meters = Total Square Feet ÷ 10.764

Nuksa ba la khein Pynbeit (Solved Example):

Lada don u plot khyndew ha Nongpoh ne Shillong ba don ka jingjrong 150 Feet bad ka jingiar 120 Feet:

  • Step 1 (Khein Sq. Feet): 150 Feet × 120 Feet = 18,000 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.).
  • Step 2 (Kylla ha Sq. Meters): 18,000 ÷ 10.764 = 1,672.2 Square Meters.
  • Step 3 (Kylla ha Acre): 18,000 ÷ 43,560 = 0.413 Acres.
  • Step 4 (Kylla ha Hectare): 18,000 ÷ 1,07,639 = 0.167 Hectares.

Ki Kyntien Shnong bad Aiñ Synshar Meghalaya 2026

Ki kyntien pyniaid khyndew bad ka aiñ District Council / Land Revenue Department Meghalaya:

  • Ri-Kynti: Ka khyndew ba don kynja trai shimet (Individual Property) ba ioh da ka Land Holding Certificate (LHC).
  • Ri-Raid: Ka khyndew shnong/syiem ba hap ha ka jingpyniaid da ka Dorbar Shnong ne District Council.
  • Land Holding Certificate (LHC): Ka syrnot sorkar ne KHADC ba pynskhem ia ka bor trai jong u briew ha ka khyndew.
  • Meghalaya Transfer of Land (Regulation) Act: Ka aiñ ba yada ia ki trai riewdah (Tribals) ban ym die khyndew ha ki mynder ri.

Ki Aiñ ba Thymmai 2026: Ha u snem 2026, ka sorkar Meghalaya ryngkat bad ki District Councils la pynjanai ia ka Bhu-Naksha Digital Mapping system bad GIS Survey ban khein pynbeit ia ki pud ki sam kumba shat ha ki Drone camera.

Kylli bad Iaathuh (FAQ)

Kylli: Kiei ki katno Square Feet ki don ha ka 1 Acre ha Meghalaya?
Jawab: Ha Meghalaya, 1 Acre ka kylla long ba ryngkat bad 43,560 Square Feet (4,046.86 Square Meters).
Kylli: Hato u non-tribal u lah ban thied Ri-Kynti khyndew?
Jawab: Katkum ka Meghalaya Land Transfer Act, ki non-tribals kim lah ban thied khyndew beit lymda ioh bor kyrpang na ka Sorkar Meghalaya.
Kylli: Katno Square Feet ki don ha 1 Hectare?
Jawab: 1 Hectare ka don ryngkat bad 1,07,639 Square Feet (ne 2.47 Acres).
Kylli: Katno Square Meters ki don ha ka 1 Square Feet?
Jawab: 1 Square Feet ka don ryngkat bad 0.0929 Square Meters.
